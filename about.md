# Abridged Bio
I'm a strategy consultant turned researcher. I spent 5+ years at KPMG Brazil (Trainee → Senior Consultant), working on market assessment, due diligence, and post-merger integration across financial services, energy, automotive, and industrial sectors.

I'm currently pursuing an academic Master's (CAPES scholarship) at FEA-RP/USP, researching Smart Disclosure: how companies can share structured data to help consumers make better decisions. I hold an MBA in Data Science and Analytics (ESALQ/USP) and a Bachelor's in Business Administration (FEA/USP, Ribeirão Preto).

I'm drawn to the intersection of information transparency, data, and consumer decision-making.
